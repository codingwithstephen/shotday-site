Privacy policy

In short: ShotDay doesn't collect any of your data.

Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What you enter

ShotDay stores the information you choose to enter: your medication name and dose, your injection schedule, the shots you log (date, dose, site and notes), side effects, and your weight. This information is stored only on your device, in ShotDay's private app storage. We (the developer) never receive it and have no way to see it.

Backups

Like other apps' data, ShotDay's data is included in your iPhone backups if you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer. Those backups are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us.

Sharing

ShotDay only shares data when you choose to, using the "Share summary with your doctor" feature. The summary goes only where you send it (for example, Messages or Mail).

Notifications

Shot-day reminders are scheduled on your device. They don't use a server and no information about them is sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

Data we collect

None. ShotDay contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking code, and doesn't link your information to you or to other companies' data.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual shots in the app. Deleting ShotDay from your iPhone permanently deletes all of its data on that device.

Children

ShotDay is intended for adults managing a prescribed medication. It isn't directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. If we ever start collecting data, we'll ask for your consent in the app first.

Not medical advice

ShotDay is a personal logging tool. It doesn't provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it never calculates, recommends or changes doses. Always follow the instructions of your prescriber and pharmacist. If you have severe or worrying symptoms, contact your doctor or emergency services. Don't rely on ShotDay to alert anyone for you.

Reminders

Reminders depend on your device's notification settings and may not always be delivered. You remain responsible for taking your medication as prescribed.

ShotDay Pro subscriptions

  • ShotDay Pro is available as an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly or yearly at the price shown in the app.
  • New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. If you don't cancel before it ends, the subscription starts and you are charged.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when the free trial ends.
  • The subscription renews automatically unless you turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies.
